# Preregistered frontier-model review protocol

**Internal registration:** 2026-08-04, before receipt of any of the three
reports; not independently time-sealed in the public history  
**Target:** prospective v1.3 quantitative I3322 dimension theorem  
**Repository branch:** `codex/quantitative-dimension-law`

## Independence contract

- Three fresh frontier-model contexts receive no conversation history and no
  prior verdict.
- Each context has a distinct attack surface and read-only instructions.
- No reviewer may edit the shared repository.
- Reports are compared only after all three have returned.
- Agreement is not counted as proof.  Every adverse or favorable conclusion
  must be supported by a checkable mathematical argument or custody fact.

This is procedural independence, not ontological independence: the reviewers
share a broad frontier-model lineage and may share training priors.  The
protocol reduces cross-report contamination but does not erase correlated
blind spots.  The project accepts this process because human referee access
is unavailable; mathematical claims still stand or fall on reproducible
arguments and certificates, not reviewer authority.

## Lanes

### Q -- quantifier and asymptotic audit

Must attempt to break:

- compact attainment of `Q_d` over mixed states and binary POVMs;
- same-dimension pure/projective reduction without dilation;
- constructive finite-section bound for odd and even `d`;
- inversion to `D(epsilon)=Theta(log(1/epsilon))`; and
- normalization and manuscript claim scope.

### P -- packet and multiplicity audit

Must attempt to break:

- finite positive joint spectral measures and density-free estimates;
- one-shift control over all moving frames;
- near-fixed, inactive, boundary, response, and exit charges;
- no-branch packet ancestry and per-chain rank;
- absence of a hidden chain-count factor;
- the recurrence lower bound and final case split; and
- exact constants, including `d=1` and `epsilon<=1` handling.

### I -- independence and custody audit

Must attempt to break:

- production/independent implementation separation;
- sealed-source blind reconstruction custody;
- manifest completeness and private-path exclusion;
- exact constant reconstruction;
- prospective v1.3 metadata status; and
- priority and claim-boundary language.

## Verdict classes

- **PASS:** no identified gap after the assigned obligations are substantively
  checked.
- **CORRECTABLE:** the mathematical core survives, but a statement,
  hypothesis, custody claim, or reproducibility artifact must be repaired.
- **FAIL:** a load-bearing implication is false or unproved under the public
  hypotheses.

## Adjudication rule

1. Reproduce every claimed `FAIL` or `CORRECTABLE` issue independently.
2. If reproducible, repair it before any release action and rerun the complete
   release verifier.
3. If not reproducible, record the attempted counterargument and the exact
   reason it fails; do not silently discard the report.
4. A `PASS` counts only for the lane obligations it actually discusses.
5. Any unaddressed load-bearing obligation remains **UNRESOLVED**, regardless
   of the headline verdict.
6. Do not tag or mint v1.3 while a reproducible `FAIL`, an unrepaired
   `CORRECTABLE`, or a load-bearing `UNRESOLVED` item remains.

## Predicted exposure before reports

The highest-risk lane is **P**, specifically the passage from arbitrarily
many moving spectral packets to a dimension-free summed energy estimate.  The
next risk is the simultaneous-shift/multi-frame multiplicity ledger.  The
same-dimension convexity reduction in **Q** is expected to survive.  Lane
**I** is expected to find at most wording or custody limitations, not a
numerical-certificate failure.
