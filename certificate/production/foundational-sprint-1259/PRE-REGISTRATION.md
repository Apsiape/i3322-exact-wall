# Pre-registration: synchronized-prefix recovery

The monotone-fibre vertical bill is invisible to ordinary optimal transport
but should be recoverable from the complete ordered flag.  Register the
finite theorem before using I3322 receipts.

For ordered source fibres with tail differences `r_i(L)` and synchronized
prefixes `R_j(L)=sum_{i<=j}r_i(L)`, prove on the full cut window that

```text
sum_i mu_i([-H,0]) |p_i-q_i|
 <=2n integral max_j |R_j(L)| dL.
```

The proof may use only tail monotonicity, the translation identity, and
finite differencing.  It may not assume atom spacing, densities, equal fibre
masses, or that the I3322 operator receipts already control `R_j`.
