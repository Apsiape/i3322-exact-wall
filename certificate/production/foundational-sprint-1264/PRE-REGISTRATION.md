# Pre-registration: output-cell sign coherence

The source-cell theorem does not by itself prevent two different source cells
inside one common output cell from carrying opposite vertical orientations.
Register the stronger I3322 target.

Use the certified predecessor derivative box to prove that the Alice response
reversal has inverse Lipschitz constant at most `20`.  Then show that within a
common output cell of width `delta`, the shift difference `p(y)-q(u)` varies
by at most `574 delta`.

For horizontally small events, choose

```text
delta<=25m_0/97006.
```

The vertical shift must then have one sign throughout the entire output cell,
even across different source cells.  No spectral-spacing assumption is
allowed.
