# Sprint 1241 pre-registration -- marginal-volume doppelganger

Date: 2026-08-04

## Question

Do the two marginal singular-spectrum/soft-volume balances of Sprint 1240,
together with positive reciprocal cocycles and finite involutions, determine
the relative closure needed by the I3322 equality proof?

## Registered prediction

No. A finite diagonal Schmidt operator should admit two different involutive
permutations. Each permutation will induce a positive reciprocal multiplier
whose weighted Schmidt operator has exactly the original singular multiset.
Thus every marginal exterior-power and every `Phi_t` balance will vanish,
while the two transported targets remain different.

## Failure conditions

- no exact finite doppelganger exists;
- positivity or reciprocal cocycle identities fail;
- the two marginal characteristic polynomials differ;
- or the target mismatch vanishes.

## Consequence if the prediction lands

The final theorem cannot use marginal determinant data alone. It must retain
the relative ordered flag of the two correspondences. The next candidate is
a regularized Schmidt-support measure on cumulative spectral projections,
not another scalar packet mass.
