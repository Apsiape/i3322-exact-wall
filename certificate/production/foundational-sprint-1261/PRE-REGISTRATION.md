# Pre-registration: sign-coherent vertical cells

Inside a common source cell, split events according to whether their actual
horizontal response displacement is at least

```text
theta=m_0/(4*(182/5)).
```

Register the prediction that the complementary events have vertical shift
`|p-q|>=25m_0/169`, and that the already certified cell width
`h<=25m_0/41769` makes the sign of `p-q` constant on that complement.

The theorem must explicitly bill horizontally large events, including their
possible cancellation of the vertical tail residual.  It may not discard
them or assume that a cell contains one spectral atom.
