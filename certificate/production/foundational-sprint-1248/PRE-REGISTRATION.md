# Pre-registration: rectangle reconstruction and the honest boundary

Sprint 1247 lifts the Schmidt data to a positive measure on ordered contact
coordinate and logarithmic resolution.  Test whether all of Sprints
1240--1245 are recoverable as literal marginals or tails of that measure.

Registered targets:

1. A vertical upper tail beginning at `log(t)` must equal `W_t` exactly.
2. Ordered cumulative soft flags must be rectangle masses, not merely an
   analogy.
3. For a normalized state the captured tail has a dimension-independent
   lower mass `1/(1+t)` and upper mass `min(rank,1/t)`.
4. Truncating resolution must expose an explicit horizontal boundary at
   `log(t)`; no proof may discard that complement without charging this flux.
5. If any orientation of the vertical tail other than `[log(t),infinity)`
   reproduces `W_t`, the event interpretation is rejected.
