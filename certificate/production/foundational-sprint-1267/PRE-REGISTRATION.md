# Pre-registration: finite-difference before removing bad addresses

The remaining localization must not bound every discarded complement inside
every cumulative prefix.  That would multiply one bad event by the number of
occupied fibres.

Register the opposite order of operations on a finite cut window:

1. apply the two full operator response receipts to every cumulative grid
   prefix;
2. finite-difference those full prefix residuals into cell residuals;
3. telescope the two response cell residuals through the original
   Alice--Bob middle term; and only then
4. remove events that fail either the source or output common address.

The target inequality must charge:

```text
sum of localized good-cell tail discrepancies
 <=2 (sum of full Alice/Bob prefix receipts)
   +C W (source/output bad mass),
```

where `W` is the finite cut-window length and `C` is an absolute constant
independent of the number of cells.  The theorem dies if the bad-mass term
inherits the cell-count factor.
