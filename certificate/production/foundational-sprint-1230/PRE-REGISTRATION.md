# Sprint 1230 pre-registration

For chains beginning in one initial slice, register the reverse endpoint
bound

```text
sum_i [z_exit(i)^2+sum_k s_(k,i)^2]
 >= W_initial / [(d+1) M^(2d)].
```

Hypotheses: every chain has at most `d` good sites, both `c` and `1/c` are at
most `M`, and `W_initial=sum_i z_(0,i)^2`. The result must not depend on the
number of chains.
