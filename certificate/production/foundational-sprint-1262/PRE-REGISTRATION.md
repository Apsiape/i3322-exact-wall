# Pre-registration: trace-normalized upper cap

Before restricting the event measure to a finite vertical band, register the
upper-boundary target.  For every density operator and `S>=0`, prove

```text
mu({zeta>=S})<=exp(-S).
```

If a response shift has magnitude at most `B`, every event that can cross the
upper boundary `S` must lie above `S-B`, so its total possible crossing mass
must be at most `exp(B-S)` per response.

The bound must use `Tr(rho)=1`, not rank, a smallest eigenvalue, or a fitted
spectral density.
