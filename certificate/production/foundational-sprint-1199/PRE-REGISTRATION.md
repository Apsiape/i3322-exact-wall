# Preregistration -- commuting-operator extension

Status: **registered before a promoted proof or verification engine**

Sprint 1197 scoped its three-remainder certificate to tensor-product quantum
strategies.  Inspection after Sprint 1198 suggests that tensor factorization
may never be used.

## Registered target

Prove or refute all three statements.

1. `R_0>=0` requires only `[X,U]=0`, so joint functional calculus applies in
   a single commuting-operator representation.
2. `R_A,R_B>=0` require only that each opposite-party response lies in the
   commutant of the corresponding two-projection algebra; the CS determinant
   proof must survive with operator-valued multiplicity fibers.
3. The Sprint-1198 finite-support equality classification uses only the joint
   spectrum of commuting `X,U` and therefore also forbids finite-dimensional
   commuting-operator attainment.

If all three land, the commuting-operator supremum equals the same `q_*`, and
no finite-dimensional commuting representation attains it.

## Falsifiers

- A local response remainder is positive on tensor products but not in a
  general commutant representation.
- The polar involution of `Y` or `V` fails to commute with the opposite-party
  response on an equality-support summand.
- Joint spectral support no longer identifies a unique scalar contact.
- Projectivizing binary commuting measurements requires a tensor factorization
  not available in the commuting model.
- The algebraic sum of the three remainders changes outside a spatial tensor
  product.

## Boundary

This campaign does not address nonspatial correlations beyond the commuting
operator model, Connes embedding, experimental realization, or corpus
metaphysics.  If the extension lands, it is a stronger Bell-operator theorem,
not a physical-foundations promotion.
