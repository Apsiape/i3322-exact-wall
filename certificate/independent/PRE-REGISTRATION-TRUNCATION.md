# Pre-registration -- independent truncation-rate reconstruction

## Separation requirement

This engine may read only the independently reconstructed plateau enclosure
in `plateau-series-mpmath.json`.  It may not import any production module or
private-corpus file.  The finite-section identity must be derived symbolically
from a generic Jacobi eigenvector equation, not copied as a numerical formula.

## Registered gates

1. Symbolic principal-section telescoping must leave exactly the two crossing
   edge fluxes for at least three independently assembled path sizes and
   intervals.
2. Omitting either lawful crossing term must leave a nonzero symbolic
   polynomial.
3. The plateau ratio must be derived from the stationary Jacobi equations and
   independently enclosed using only the `mpmath.iv` plateau interval.
4. The enclosure must contain `1.07809205080209208` and lie strictly above
   one.
5. The resulting `log(R)` enclosure must contain
   `0.07519285919570202` and lie strictly above zero.

## Claim boundary

Passing proves an independent reconstruction of the algebraic truncation
identity and its certified exponent input.  The analytic passage from the
certified wall's geometric tails to the limit law remains a written proof.
Nothing here supplies a lower bound on arbitrary dimension-`d` strategies.
