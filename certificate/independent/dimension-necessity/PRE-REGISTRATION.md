Timestamp context: 2026-08-04, before substantive derivation.

## Fixed proof attempt

For a pure projective dimension-`d` optimizer with deficit `epsilon`, I will:

1. use the saturated contact coordinates to split a retained compact response
   box into a near-fixed sector and two oriented drift sectors;
2. choose dimension-dependent mesh
   `h_d = Delta/(4*20^d)` and average one shift over the actual one-sided
   histories only, retaining all frame multiplicities;
3. charge near-fixed captured mass with the explicit Sprint 1229 inequality,
   and charge its source/target omissions from the same contact and response
   debts with stated multiplicity;
4. start all drift chains from one shifted spectral slice and follow exact
   same-index moving-frame addresses until zero projection, drift exit,
   near-fixed entry, or time `d`;
5. prove directly that each physical packet has one predecessor and at most one
   successor, and that distinct packets in a fixed frame are orthogonal;
6. upper-bound recurrence and terminal energy in terms of the three certificate
   debts, including every reuse in time/frame;
7. compare this upper bound with the finite-rank reverse endpoint lower bound
   using `M=78/5`;
8. solve the resulting inequality without importing any rumored exponential
   base.

## Pre-registered failure test

If the stated two-frame, rounding, near-fixed, and exit lemmas do not actually
imply a global terminal/recurrence upper bound for the constructed ancestry
family, the verdict will be `FAIL` at that first arrow. In particular I will
not infer transport-closure merely from per-frame pairings, nor infer that the
sum of all chain lengths is at most `d`.

## Constant policy

Conservative rational enlargements are allowed and will be shown. Numerical
experiments, if any, are checks only. The final base will be derived from the
assembled powers of `20`, `78/5`, and any explicitly introduced fixed
constants.

measures, Tonelli for nonnegative functions, spectral-calculus orthogonality,
