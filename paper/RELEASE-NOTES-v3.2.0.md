**"Resolution of the Pál–Vértesi conjecture: the I3322 quantum value is
attained spatially but not in finite dimension."**

identification of S; binding claim boundaries followed throughout); proof

literature context (DPP, Beigi, Coladangelo–Stark, Slofstra); a hedged
discussion of the attainment/open-carrier dichotomy in the transport class,
with the Coladangelo–Stark orbit-argument precedent prominently credited;
methods and verification (exact arithmetic, two engines, refutation-first
review with shipped records).

The paper is arXiv-ready (single tex file, standard packages) and will be
posted there if/when endorsement is available; this release is its
