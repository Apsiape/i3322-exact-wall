\begin{abstract}
Let $S$ denote the quantum ($=$ commuting) value of the $I_{3322}$ Bell
functional in the Collins--Gisin normalization (classical bound $0$; qubit
and qutrit maximum exactly $1/4$), known through the certified window
$S\in(0.2508753845015185,\,0.250875388108398]$. The companion paper
\cite{D26} proves that $S$ is not attained by any finite-dimensional
strategy but is attained by a normal spatial strategy on
$\ell^2(\mathbb{Z})\otimes\ell^2(\mathbb{Z})$, so
$\Cqs(3,3;2,2)\setminus\Cq(3,3;2,2)\neq\emptyset$. This note answers the
quantitative question those results open: how fast must local dimension
grow as a strategy approaches $S$? Writing $S_d$ for the supremum over
strategies whose projective measurements act on local factors of dimension
at most $d$ (no dilation; pure states), and
$D(\eps)=\min\{\,d : S-S_d\le\eps\,\}$, we prove

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Theorem.} \emph{$D(\eps)=\Theta(\log(1/\eps))$, with
existential constants; the upper half carries the explicit derived bound
$\limsup_{\eps\downarrow0} D(\eps)/\log(1/\eps)\le 1/\keff\le
23.9010650$.}
\smallskip

The upper bound is constructive: an explicit endpoint-projector truncation
of the attaining $\ell^2(\mathbb{Z})$ carrier reaches accuracy $\eps$ at
local dimension exactly $d=|I|$ for a window $I$ of length
$O(\log(1/\eps))$. The lower bound shows every $d$-dimensional strategy is
separated from $S$ by at least $c(1+d)^{-p}e^{-Cd}$. The combinatorial and
scalar cores of both halves are machine-checked in Lean~4 (27 theorems,
standard axioms only); the reductions and assembly are carried by the
certificate documents that accompany this note in the public repository.
To our knowledge (a search receipt ships with the repository) this is the
first two-sided ($\Theta$) dimension-complexity bound for a proven
$\Cqs\setminus\Cq$ witness: the value $S$ is not merely unattainable in
finite dimension --- the cost of approaching it is itself a theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The $I_{3322}$ inequality is the simplest bipartite Bell functional beyond
the CHSH family, with three binary measurements per party. Unlike CHSH ---
whose quantum value is attained by a qubit pair --- $I_{3322}$ has long
been observed numerically to reward increasing local dimension
\cite{PV10}. The companion paper \cite{D26} converts that observation into
theorems: the quantum value $S$ is \emph{not attained} at any finite local
dimension (Theorem~1 there), yet \emph{is attained} by a normal spatial
strategy on $\ell^2(\mathbb{Z})\otimes\ell^2(\mathbb{Z})$ (Theorem~2),
exhibiting a correlation in $\Cqs(3,3;2,2)\setminus\Cq(3,3;2,2)$ and
resolving the closure question at the $(3,3;2,2)$ scenario; see
\cite{DPP19,CS18,Sl19,MR20} for the separation landscape this sits in, and
\cite{Co17} for dimension witnesses, the broader tradition of paying for
correlation strength in Hilbert-space dimension.

Nonattainment makes $\mathrm{gap}(d):=S-S_d$ strictly positive for every
$d$ (Section~2). How fast does it decay? The theorem places $I_{3322}$ in
the geometric regime with matching halves: for all sufficiently small
$\eps$,
\[
c_1\log(1/\eps)\ \le\ D(\eps)\ \le\ c_2\log(1/\eps),
\]
and the upper half comes with the explicit safe constant
$c_2\le 23.9010650$ (Section~4; a derived over-estimate --- no sharpness
is claimed for it). The a priori alternatives split cleanly: either
$D(\eps)=\Theta(\log(1/\eps))$ --- geometric convergence --- or
$D(\eps)/\log(1/\eps)\to\infty$, whose extreme case, polynomial dimension
cost $D(\eps)\sim\eps^{-c}$, would have made $\eps$-accuracy exponentially
expensive in digits of precision. The theorem selects the first
alternative, from both halves at once: windowing the attaining carrier
converges geometrically, and --- by the lower bound --- nothing converges
faster than geometrically.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Relation to the companion results and to prior partial
rates.} Both halves consume the companion paper's machinery: the upper
bound truncates the Theorem-2 carrier; the lower bound plays finite
strategies against the same scalar response structure that forces
nonattainment. The repository's earlier dimension-gap document
\cite{DGS26} had already established a constructive one-sided rate for the
specific carrier family (an asymptotically exact carrier exponent
$\log R = 0.07519285919570202\ldots$, $R = 1.07809205080209208\ldots$, so
$\log(1/\eps)/\log R + O(1)$ local dimensions suffice along that family)
together with a \emph{conditional} lower bound whose missing step it named
explicitly. This note supersedes that document on the lower side ---
\S3 is the unconditional discharge of the gap \cite{DGS26} named --- and
generalizes it on the upper: the uniform constant
$1/\keff\le 23.9010650$ here is weaker but carrier-uniform, consistent
with, and not claiming, \cite{DGS26}'s sharper family-specific exponent.
All consumed statements carry hash anchors into the certificate repository
(Section~6).

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Scope.} Local Hilbert-space dimension; projective
measurements and pure states (see Section~2 for why this class is the
right one and Section~5 for the fence); all constants existential except
the displayed upper constant, which is a derived over-estimate. The
companion certificate's disclosed residual risk and the standing of each
consumed receipt are inherited and flagged exactly where they are consumed
(Sections~4.1 and~5).

\section{Setting and statement}

Fix the $I_{3322}$ Bell operator in the Collins--Gisin normalization
\cite{CG04},
\begin{equation*}
\B \;=\; -A_2-B_1-2B_2
\;+\;A_1B_1+A_1B_2-A_1B_3
\;+\;A_2B_1+A_2B_2+A_2B_3
\;-\;A_3B_1+A_3B_2 ,
\end{equation*}
with three binary projection-valued measurements per party (marginal
coefficients of absolute sum $4$; eight joint coefficients of modulus
$1$). Classical bound $0$; qubit and qutrit maximum exactly $1/4$
\cite{CG04,PV10}; and
\begin{equation}\tag{window}
S \;\in\; (0.2508753845015185,\; 0.250875388108398]
\end{equation}
with $S:=\omega_{\mathrm{tensor}}(I_{3322})
=\omega_{\mathrm{commuting}}(I_{3322})$ \cite{D26}. Write
$S_{\mathrm{LO}}:=0.2508753845015185$; the window is open at its lower
endpoint, so $S>S_{\mathrm{LO}}>1/4$ strictly. Define, for each $d\ge1$,
\begin{align*}
S_d &:= \sup\,\bigl\{\,\langle\psi|\B|\psi\rangle \;:\;
\dim\mathcal{H}_A\le d,\ \dim\mathcal{H}_B\le d,\ \text{three
projection-valued binary}\\
&\hphantom{{}:={}\sup\,\bigl\{\,}\text{measurements per party on }
\mathcal{H}_A,\ \mathcal{H}_B,\ \psi\in\mathcal{H}_A\otimes\mathcal{H}_B
\text{ a unit vector}\,\bigr\},\\
D(\eps) &:= \min\,\{\, d\in\mathbb{N} \;:\; S-S_d\le\eps \,\}.
\end{align*}
The parameter set is compact --- a finite product of projection varieties
on $\mathbb{C}^d$ together with the unit sphere of
$\mathbb{C}^d\otimes\mathbb{C}^d$ --- and the value is continuous, so the
supremum defining $S_d$ is attained; Theorem~1 of \cite{D26} therefore
gives $S_d<S$ for every $d$. $S_d$ is nondecreasing, and $S_d\to S$ by the
construction of Section~4, so $D(\eps)$ is well-defined and finite for
every $\eps>0$.

\medskip
\noindent\emph{Scope of $S_d$.} Projective measurements and pure states,
at local-dimension scope with no dilation. Theorem~1 of \cite{D26} covers
POVMs and mixed states, and by compactness and multilinear extremality the
$d$-dimensional optimum is achieved by a pure state with projective
measurements on the same local spaces \cite{DGS26}, so this class loses no
generality for the \emph{value}; since Naimark dilation increases local
dimension, no separate POVM-indexed rate is claimed (Section~5).

\begin{thmrate}[rate]
There are constants $c_1,c_2>0$ with
$c_1\log(1/\eps)\le D(\eps)\le c_2\log(1/\eps)$ for all sufficiently
small $\eps>0$. The upper half holds with the explicit derived constant:
$\limsup_{\eps\downarrow0} D(\eps)/\log(1/\eps)\le 1/\keff\le
23.9010650$.
\end{thmrate}

Equivalently at the level of the gap: for every $0<C_1<\keff$ there is
$d_0$ with $S-S_d\le e^{-C_1 d}$ for all $d\ge d_0$; and there are
$c,p,C>0$ with $S-S_d\ge c(1+d)^{-p}e^{-Cd}$ for every $d$.

\section{The lower bound: \texorpdfstring{$D(\eps)=\Omega(\log(1/\eps))$}{D(eps) = Omega(log(1/eps))}}

The lower half says no finite-dimensional strategy --- windowed or not ---
approaches $S$ faster than geometrically. \textbf{This section is a guide
to the proof, not the proof: the complete argument is the certificate
chain \cite{RLB26} (five sealed --- hash-frozen and audit-locked ---
documents, cited by number below), and the three combinatorial cores on
which it turns are machine-checked in Lean~4 (Section~6).} The statement
established by that chain (whose audited promotion record ships alongside
it) is
\begin{equation}\tag{3.1}\label{eq:lb}
S-S_d \;\ge\; c(1+d)^{-p}\,e^{-Cd}\qquad\text{for every } d,
\end{equation}
with existential $c,p,C>0$, which gives
$D(\eps)\ge c_1\log(1/\eps)-O(1)$. The architecture, in the order the
sealed chain runs it:

\subsection{From an \texorpdfstring{$\eps$}{eps}-good strategy to a small response defect}

A $d$-dimensional strategy within $\eps$ of $S$ induces, through the
companion paper's scalar reduction (the same reduction driving Theorem~1),
a finite family of scalar labels on spectral cells satisfying the
same-marginal response identities up to a global defect $E$ bounded
polynomially in $\eps$: after the far-inversion and endpoint payments
(\cite{RLB26} Docs~01--02), $E\le C_0\,\eps^{1/8}$ (\cite{RLB26} Doc~05,
(5.1)). (The \emph{label-localization} modulus that appears later in the
chain is, by contrast, a compactness modulus with no claimed rate --- the
polynomial rate is claimed for $E$ only.)

\subsection{A small defect localizes an approximate fixed structure}

By finiteness, the label family under the (monotone) response recursion
contains a repeated cell --- a $\delta$-pseudo-cycle --- and the first
machine-checked core (Lean: \texttt{min\_point\_pseudocycle}) extracts
from any cyclic $\delta$-pseudo-orbit of a monotone real map a
$\delta$-approximate fixed point at its minimum, with the \emph{same}
accuracy $\delta$, independent of cycle length. The sealed chain's
min-point squeeze (\cite{RLB26} Doc~03) refines this to a \emph{reflected
zero pair} $(z,-z)$ of the response structure, by compactness (no rate at
this step).

\subsection{The quarter obstruction repels the fixed structure}

At the reflected pair the chain derives neutral gain --- the two parties'
normalized transport multiplier gains coincide (\cite{RLB26} Doc~04,
(4.1)) --- which --- combined with the identification $Q=S$ established
beforehand (\cite{RLB26} Doc~04, (5.3)) --- forces the distorted-return
value $F(1)=1/4$ for $S$ itself, contradicting the certified $S>1/4$.
(Here $F(q)=(1+q)^2/(16q)$ is the sealed chain's distorted-return
functional (equivalently $1/4+(1-q)^2/(16q)$); only the qualitative fact
$S>1/4$ is consumed, not the window's numerical margin.) By
contraposition, an $\eps$-good strategy's labels can only \emph{approach}
the reflected configuration at a price: Doc~04's dimension-free constant
bounds the approach in terms of the defect, $m_C^2\le C_C\,E$
(\cite{RLB26} Doc~04, (6.1)).

\subsection{Dimension caps the escape}

The second machine-checked core (Lean: \texttt{staircase\_card\_le}, via
\texttt{staircase\_sum\_injOn}) is the staircase edge budget: an
inversion-free bipartite relation on
$\texttt{Fin}\,m\times\texttt{Fin}\,n$ has at most $m+n-1$ edges. After
far-inversion deletion each parity class of the retained cell support is
inversion-free, so a $d$-dimensional strategy's usable response graph has
at most $4d-1$ edges and its response walks have length at most $2d$
(\cite{RLB26} Doc~01 \S4, Doc~05 (1.3)). Along such a walk the chain
compounds a per-step gain, and the third core (Lean:
\texttt{prod\_le\_two\_pow\_sum}: a product of positive branch degrees is
at most two to their sum, the degree sum here being $<8d$) turns the
walk's branch degrees into the Green-product floor
$\Gamma_d = 2^{-4d}\bar{\sigma}^{2d}$ (\cite{RLB26} Doc~02, (6.4)). The
endgame (\cite{RLB26} Doc~05 \S\S3--5) plays the two against each other: a
defect small compared to $\Gamma_d$ would let the walk reach the forbidden
reflected configuration, so $E\ge c(1+d)^{-p}e^{-Cd}$; the eighth-power
inversion of 3.1's $E\le C_0\eps^{1/8}$ then yields (3.1) (with the
polynomial prefactor absorbed into a marginally larger $C$ whenever the
cleaner $\exp(-C'd)$ form is wanted).

\section{The upper bound: \texorpdfstring{$D(\eps)=O(\log(1/\eps))$}{D(eps) = O(log(1/eps))}, explicitly}

The upper half is constructive and given here in full, modulo the
companion paper's Theorem~2 and the receipts named below.

\subsection{The carrier}

Theorem~2 of \cite{D26} supplies a positive unit vector
$\lambda\in\ell^2(\mathbb{Z})$ --- the \emph{carrier} --- and a
bi-infinite label sequence $(c_j)\subset(-1,1)$ with:
\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] the certificate's interior zero locus $Z$ (the zero set of its
scalar weld functional; \cite{D26} \S6) is the graph of a strictly
increasing one-to-one Borel map $P$, and $P(c_{j+1})=c_j$ for every $j$
--- one map, every link (\cite{D26} \S10);
\item[(ii)] the transport law $\lambda_{j+1}/\lambda_j = g(c_j)/b(c_j)$
(\cite{D26} \S11), where $b(t)=\sqrt{1-t^2}/2$ and $g$ is the
certificate's terminal-pivot storage function (the infimum of terminal
Schur pivots over finite histories; \cite{D26} Methods), with
$g\ge m_g>0$ on $[-1,1]$;
\item[(iii)] the eigen-equation $H\lambda=S\lambda$ for the Jacobi matrix
$H_{jj}=\delta(c_{j-1},c_j)$, $H_{j-1,j}=b(c_{j-1})$, where
$\delta(x,u):=xu+(x-u)/2-1$ is the certificate's diagonal cost
(\cite{D26}; the display is anchored at the repository's sprint-1197
document);
\item[(iv)] every adjacent label pair lies in $Z$, and $Z$ is compactly
interior, $Z\subset\subset(-1,1)^2$.
\end{itemize}

\medskip
\noindent\emph{Standing of the inputs, disclosed at the point of
consumption.} Item~(i) is proved in the certificate's \S\S6--9 (conull
invariant set, Borel transversal, uniqueness of disintegration) and \S10,
and the certificate's status materials flag \S\S6--9 as carrying its
residual proof risk pending their expanded write-up; this note inherits
that disclosed risk here. The positivity $g\ge m_g$ and the compact
interiority (iv) are supplied by the repository's endpoint-positivity
receipt, whose own status line --- ``PROVED CANDIDATE; promotion audit may
attack provenance/typing but no new frontier input is assumed'' --- is
likewise inherited, not upgraded (both fences are repeated in Section~5).

Let $K$ be the compact interior union of coordinate projections of the
closure of $Z$; all labels and label limits lie in $K$, and on $K$ the
functions $b$, $g$, $r:=g/b$ are continuous with $b\ge b_0>0$ and $r$,
$1/r$ bounded by $R_{\max}<\infty$.

\subsection{Monotone labels and geometric tails}

Since $P$ is strictly increasing and one-to-one with $P(c_{j+1})=c_j$ for
every $j$, the labels satisfy $c_{j+1}=P^{-1}(c_j)$. The sequence cannot
be constant: a constant label $c$ would make $\lambda$ exactly geometric
with ratio $r(c)$ --- not in $\ell^2(\mathbb{Z})$ in one direction if
$r(c)\neq1$, and a non-normalizable constant vector if $r(c)=1$. Hence
(induction with the strictly increasing $P^{-1}$ forward and $P$ backward)
the whole sequence is strictly monotone, confined to the compact $K$, so
both tail limits $t_\pm\in K$ exist, and by the transport law and
continuity of $r$ the outward one-step ratios converge:
\[
y_+ := \lim_{j\to+\infty}\lambda_{j+1}/\lambda_j = r(t_+),\qquad
y_- := \lim_{j\to-\infty}\lambda_{j-1}/\lambda_j = 1/r(t_-).
\]

Dividing row $j$ of $H\lambda=S\lambda$ by $\lambda_j$ and passing to
either limit gives
\begin{equation}\tag{4.1}\label{eq:band}
y+1/y \;=\; \mu(t) := \frac{S-\Delta(t)}{b(t)},\qquad
\Delta(t):=\delta(t,t)=t^2-1,
\end{equation}
at $t=t_\pm$. The elementary band identity --- for $s=\sqrt{1-t^2}$,
\begin{equation}\tag{4.2}\label{eq:bandid}
\Delta(t)+2b(t) \;=\; t^2-1+s \;=\; s(1-s) \;\le\; 1/4
\end{equation}
(machine-checked: \texttt{band\_identity},
\texttt{s\_mul\_one\_sub\_s\_le\_quarter},
\texttt{band\_quarter\_ceiling}) --- with $b\le1/2$
(\texttt{amplitude\_b\_le\_half}) and the certified $S>S_{\mathrm{LO}}$
yields, for every interior $t$,
\begin{equation}\tag{4.3}\label{eq:mumin}
\begin{aligned}
\mu(t)-2 \;&=\; (S-\Delta-2b)/b \;\ge\; (S-1/4)/b \;\ge\; 2(S-1/4)\\
&>\; 2(S_{\mathrm{LO}}-1/4) \;=\; 0.001750769003037,\\
\text{so }\ \mu(t) \;&\ge\; \mu_{\min} := 2.001750769003037.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Since $\ell^2$ excludes the growing root of (4.1) at each end, both
outward ratios equal the decaying root
$x_{\mathrm{dec}}(\mu)=2/(\mu+\sqrt{\mu^2-4})$, strictly decreasing in
$\mu$, so uniformly
\begin{equation}\tag{4.4}\label{eq:ydec}
y_\pm \;\le\; x_{\mathrm{dec}}(\mu_{\min}) \;\le\; 0.9590241,\qquad
y_\pm^2 \;\le\; 0.9197272 \;<\; 1.
\end{equation}
Tail mass sums amplitude squares, so with $\kappa_\pm:=-2\log y_\pm$ the
tails obey $\lambda_j^2\lesssim e^{-\kappa_\pm|j|}$, and --- carrying the
down-rounded surrogate
$K_0 := 0.08367827985 \le -2\log x_{\mathrm{dec}}(\mu_{\min})$, which is
safe both in $K_0/2$ (rounded down) and in the \emph{denominator} of
$2/K_0$ (a down-rounded denominator over-estimates the quotient, the safe
direction for an upper display):
\begin{equation}\tag{4.5}\label{eq:kappa}
\begin{aligned}
\kappa_\pm \;&\ge\; K_0 = 0.08367827985 \ \text{per index},\\
\keff \;&:=\; (1/\kappa_- + 1/\kappa_+)^{-1} \;\ge\; K_0/2 \;\ge\;
0.0418391,\\
1/\keff \;&\le\; 2/K_0 = 23.90106493088\ldots \;\le\; 23.9010650.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
(All displayed decimals are bounds rounded in the safe direction and
re-verified in exact rational arithmetic by the repository's guard
scripts --- scripts that re-derive every displayed constant from its
stated inputs and fail on any mismatch. Because
$1/\keff\le 2/K_0 = 23.90106493088\ldots$ sits strictly below
$23.9010650$ with a genuine gap, the eventual bound
$D(\eps)\le 23.9010650\cdot\log(1/\eps)$ of Section~1 follows --- a bare
$\limsup\le 23.9010650$ alone would not give it.)

\subsection{The truncation, at exact local dimension \texorpdfstring{$d$}{d}}

For a window $I=[-L,R]\cap\mathbb{Z}$, keep every full $2\times2$
rank-one block of each of the carrier's two alternating matchings whose
paired indices lie in $I$; at a severed endpoint pair, replace the block
by the one-dimensional projector on the retained index. All six
measurement operators remain exact projections --- each is a direct sum of
orthogonal rank-one blocks and one-dimensional projectors (verified
symbolically at arbitrary block angles for window sizes $m=3,\ldots,8$ by
an independently written script; the general case is the direct-sum
structure itself) --- and the local dimension is exactly $d=|I|$, with no
dilation and no padding. The truncated state is the renormalized window
of $\lambda$.

\subsection{Error accounting}

Let $v_I$ be the value of the principal Jacobi compression on $I$ and
$V_I$ the Bell value of the 4.3 strategy. The exact two-boundary flux
identity (two lines from $H\lambda=S\lambda$):
\[
S-v_I \;=\; \bigl[\,b(c_{-L-1})\lambda_{-L-1}\lambda_{-L}
+ b(c_R)\lambda_R\lambda_{R+1}\,\bigr]/M_I,
\]
with $M_I$ the window mass --- only the two cut edges are unpaid.
Relative to the compression, the completion changes exactly three kinds
of term: the two cut-bond off-diagonals (already and only accounted by
the flux identity --- not charged twice), the two retained endpoint
diagonals, and nothing else. Each of the eight joint Bell terms has
coefficient of modulus $1$, and a joint term's diagonal is a product of
two projector diagonals, so its change has modulus at most $2$; the
marginal coefficients contribute their absolute sum $4$; hence the safe
count $C_{\mathrm{diag}}\le 4\cdot1+8\cdot2=20$ and
\[
0 \;\le\; S-V_I \;\le\; (1/2 + 20\cdot R_{\max})\cdot B_I/(1-T_I),
\]
with $B_I$ the boundary products and $T_I$ the omitted tail mass. Both
decay like $e^{-\kappa_- L}+e^{-\kappa_+ R}$ by \S4.2, so balancing
$L:R$ as $\kappa_+:\kappa_-$ with $|I|=d$ exactly:
\begin{equation}\tag{4.6}\label{eq:ub}
S-S_d \;\le\; S-V_{I_d} \;\le\; \exp(-\keff\cdot d + o(d)),
\end{equation}
and therefore, for every $\eta>0$,
$D(\eps)\le(\keff-\eta)^{-1}\log(1/\eps)$ eventually; letting
$\eta\downarrow0$ gives
$\limsup D(\eps)/\log(1/\eps)\le 1/\keff\le 23.9010650$.
\hfill$\square$

\subsection{Remark (concrete dimensions)}

The geometric regime is visible at concrete sizes: the repository's
independently written verification script evaluates explicit strategies
of the P\'al--V\'ertesi family --- a lower bound on $S_d$, not this
note's 4.3 construction --- on the sampled ladder
$d\in\{3,4,6,8,12,16,24,33\}$, with values exceeding the qubit/qutrit
ceiling $1/4$ at $d=24$ and $d=33$ (high-precision evaluation with
rigorous Rayleigh-quotient lower bounds asserted by the script); by
monotonicity of $S_d$, $S_d>1/4$ for all $d\ge24$. The family is not
optimal at small $d$ and nothing is claimed about the exact crossing
point.

\section{What is and is not claimed}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Existential constants} in the $\Theta$. The single explicit
number $23.9010650$ bounds the upper half's constant only; it derives
from the certified window and the band algebra alone via a certified
exact-rational chain, and is certainly not sharp (the true carrier rate
is governed by $r$ at the actual tail limits; compare the family-specific
exponent of \cite{DGS26}). The lower half's constants $c,p,C$ are
existential and no explicit values are claimed, so no explicit two-sided
window on $D(\eps)/\log(1/\eps)$ is asserted.
\item \textbf{Scope:} local Hilbert-space dimension; projective
measurements and pure states (lossless for $S_d$ by compactness and
extremality, Section~2); no Schmidt-rank statement; no POVM-indexed rate.
\item \textbf{Asymptotic form:} the $o(d)$ in (4.6) is not made explicit;
the Theorem's inequalities are claimed for all sufficiently small $\eps$.
\item \textbf{Machine-checking boundary:} the Lean kernel checks the
combinatorial and scalar cores named in Sections~3--4 (27 theorems,
standard axioms only). The reductions, transport typing, and assembly are
\emph{not} formalized; they are carried by the certificate documents.
\item \textbf{Inheritance:} BOTH halves consume the endpoint-positivity
receipt at its stated proved-candidate standing, and both consume the
certificate's \S6/\S10 graph structure, which lies inside the disclosed
\S\S6--9 residual-risk range (Section~4.1); the carrier vector $\lambda$
itself --- Theorem~2's $\ell^2(\mathbb{Z})$ construction --- is consumed
by the upper half only. The two halves are independent in route and in
audit history, not in these shared roots.
\item \textbf{Status of Section~3:} a guide to the sealed certificate
chain, which is not reproduced in this note; the sealed documents govern.
\item \textbf{Priority:} the claim of a first two-sided ($\Theta$) bound
is made to our knowledge, with the literature-search receipt shipped in
the repository.
\end{enumerate}

and V\'ertesi's see-saw studies \cite{PV10}, which supply the family

cites the prior exact certified enclosure of Mghirbi \cite{M26}. The
repository's earlier dimension-gap document \cite{DGS26} contains the

see \cite{DPP19,CS18,Sl19,MR20}; for dimension witnesses \cite{Co17}.
