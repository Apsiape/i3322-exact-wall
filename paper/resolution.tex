\begin{abstract}
Let $S=\omega_{\mathrm{tensor}}(I_{3322})=\omega_{\mathrm{commuting}}(I_{3322})$
denote the quantum supremum of the $I_{3322}$ Bell functional in the
Collins--Gisin normalization (classical bound $0$; qubit and qutrit maximum
exactly $1/4$). We report a computer-assisted, exactly certified resolution of
the attainment questions for $S$: (i)~$S$ lies in the certified window
$(0.2508753845015185,\,0.250875388108398]$, and the tensor-product and
commuting-operator suprema coincide at one common value; (ii)~\textbf{no
finite-dimensional quantum strategy attains $S$} --- any dimension, pure or
mixed states, projective or POVM measurements --- resolving the conjecture of
P\'al and V\'ertesi (2010); (iii)~$S$ \textbf{is} attained by a normal spatial
strategy on $\ell^2(\mathbb{Z})\otimes\ell^2(\mathbb{Z})$. Consequently
$\Cq(3,3;2,2)$ is not closed --- the smallest scenario by input count in which
nonclosure is known --- and $\Cqs(3,3;2,2)\setminus \Cq(3,3;2,2)\neq\emptyset$.
The nonattainment proof runs through a canonical critical Bellman storage
(concave, obtained as a greatest feasible fixed point), exact rational
endpoint-exclusion certificates, a reflection-gluing inequality, and a
convex-envelope theorem excluding contact plateaus; finiteness then forces the
two equality transports of an exact maximizer to coincide, which caps its value
at $1/4<S$. The attainment proof disintegrates the scalar spectral measure of a
maximizing commuting state over the orbits of its response translation and
reads off an $\ell^2$ Jacobi eigenvector, with normalizability inherited from
the probability measure rather than imposed. Every load-bearing step was
audited in refutation-first adversarial review whose complete records ship
with the certificates, together with a full correction history: an earlier
claimed proof by this program was publicly decertified after an exact audit,
and nothing decertified re-enters the present chain. We discuss a structural
dichotomy suggested by the results: across the known Bell-functional zoo,
finite-dimensional attainment coincides with closed (finitely recurrent)
optimal carriers, while every known transport-class nonattainment is carried
by an infinite open chain.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The $I_{3322}$ inequality \cite{Froissart,CollinsGisin} is the best-known
bipartite Bell functional beyond the Clauser--Horne--Shimony--Holt family: three
binary measurements per party, and a facet of the local polytope inequivalent
to CHSH. In 2010, P\'al and V\'ertesi \cite{PalVertesi} computed its quantum
value numerically to high precision, exhibited a family of finite-dimensional
strategies converging to $0.2508753845\ldots$, observed that qubit strategies
reach exactly $1/4$ and that local dimension at least $12$ is needed to exceed
it, and conjectured that \emph{no finite dimension suffices}: the supremum
should be attained only in infinite dimension. The conjecture has stood since.
Coladangelo and Stark, in constructing the first explicit bipartite correlation
attainable only in infinite dimension (on larger alphabets: $4{\times}5$
inputs, $3$ outputs), wrote of the $I_{3322}$ case that ``an analytical proof
has remained elusive'' \cite{ColadangeloStark}.

This paper reports that resolution, in both directions, by computer-assisted
proof with exact certificates:

\begin{theorem}[Nonattainment]\label{thm:N}
In the Collins--Gisin normalization, let
$S=\omega_{\mathrm{tensor}}(I_{3322})=\omega_{\mathrm{commuting}}(I_{3322})$
be the quantum supremum, certified to lie in
$(0.2508753845015185,\,0.250875388108398]$. Then no finite-dimensional quantum
strategy attains $S$: for every finite dimension $d$, every (pure or mixed)
state on $\mathbb{C}^d\otimes\mathbb{C}^d$ and every triple of binary
measurements per party (projective or POVM),
$\langle I_{3322}\rangle < S$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Spatial attainment]\label{thm:S}
$S$ is attained by a normal spatial strategy: there is a unit vector
$\psi_S\in\ell^2(\mathbb{Z})\otimes\ell^2(\mathbb{Z})$ and six projective
binary measurements built from the alternating P\'al--V\'ertesi projector
blocks with $\langle\psi_S,\B_{3322}\,\psi_S\rangle=S$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:nonclosure}
$\Cq(3,3;2,2)$ is not closed, and
$\Cqs(3,3;2,2)\setminus \Cq(3,3;2,2)\neq\emptyset$.
\end{corollary}

By input count, $(3,3;2,2)$ is the smallest scenario in which nonclosure of
the finite-dimensional quantum correlation set is known: the previously
smallest examples are the $(5,5;2,2)$ synchronous construction of
Dykema--Paulsen--Prakash \cite{DPP}, the $(4,4;2,2)$ and $(4,4;3,3)$
separations of Beigi \cite{Beigi}, the $(4,5;3,3)$ correlation of
Coladangelo--Stark \cite{ColadangeloStark}, and Slofstra's original
$184{\times}235$-input games \cite{Slofstra1,Slofstra2}. (In the $(2,2;2,2)$
scenario every functional attains its maximum on two qubits by Jordan's lemma
\cite{Masanes}, so three inputs is also the smallest possible venue for this
phenomenon.)

\subsection*{Correction history, stated first}

This program previously released a claimed proof of the $I_{3322}$ headline
(exact optimum, spatial attainment, nonattainment) whose load-bearing global
amplitude datum was subsequently \emph{refuted by our own post-release exact
audit}: the amplitude-compatibility equation of the original bi-infinite wall
construction fails by an exactly certified margin (the mismatch lies in
$[1.40\times10^{-4},1.79\times10^{-4}]$, excluding zero). The claims were
publicly decertified within a day, and the repository's release history
preserves the full correction record. The results announced here were then
rebuilt on independent routes; the decertified fixed point, amplitude profile,
and tail constants appear on explicit \emph{not-used} lists in the certificate
dependency records, and the failed compatibility equation is not repaired
anywhere in the present chain --- the new attainment route never poses it
(Section~\ref{sec:S}). Every load-bearing theorem below then survived three
rounds (Theorem~\ref{thm:N}) plus one round (Theorem~\ref{thm:S}) of
independent, refutation-first adversarial review; the signed final review
records ship with the certificates \cite{Repo}. We state this history first
because computer-assisted claims are only as credible as their failure
handling; the correction record is part of the result.

\section{Setting and certified inputs}\label{sec:setup}

We use the Collins--Gisin normalization of $I_{3322}$ \cite{CollinsGisin,
PalVertesi}: the classical (local deterministic) bound is $0$; the maximum over
two-qubit --- indeed, by \cite{PalVertesi}, over qubit and qutrit ---
strategies is exactly $1/4$; P\'al--V\'ertesi report that local dimension
$\ge 12$ is required to exceed $1/4$, with their strategy family converging to
$0.250875384514\ldots$ \cite{PalVertesi}. Throughout, $S$ denotes the common
quantum value of Theorem~\ref{thm:N}, identified \emph{only} by its certified
window; we do not claim $S$ equals the historical decimal, nor that the
P\'al--V\'ertesi family is optimal, though all certified data are consistent
with both.

The proofs consume the following exactly certified inputs, established in the
public repository \cite{Repo} (certificate directories cited in
Section~\ref{sec:methods}): (T0)~a Bellman/path variational theorem giving
$\omega_{\mathrm{tensor}}=\omega_{\mathrm{commuting}}=S$, together with, for
every level $q>S$, a terminal-pivot storage $g_q$ --- the infimum of terminal
Schur pivots over finite histories --- which is positive, Bellman-feasible, and
equicontinuous in $q$; (LB)~an exact $255$-dimensional strategy certifying
$S>0.2508753845015185>1/4$ (rational square-root floors, independently
reconstructed in $160$-digit interval arithmetic); (UB)~an exact rational
$25{,}601$-knot Bellman witness certifying $S\le 0.250875388108398$; and
(W)~a generic operator weld: any positive Bellman-feasible storage at level
$q$ yields a positive decomposition $qI-\B=R_0+R_A+R_B$ with the local
remainder structure of \cite{Repo} (sprint 1197). The finite
P\'al--V\'ertesi block-to-Jacobi identity (\cite{Repo}, sprint 1206,
\S\S2--4 only --- the finite, endpoint-free form) supplies the bridge between
Jacobi data and Bell strategies; the adversarial review independently
replicated it at machine precision.

\section{Nonattainment}\label{sec:N}

We sketch the architecture; complete proofs, with every constant exact, are in
the certificate documents \cite{Repo}, directory
\texttt{theorem-N-four-receipts-at-S}.

\emph{The canonical critical storage.} The map $q\mapsto g_q$ is monotone:
increasing the level by $\eta$ increases every terminal Schur pivot by at
least $\eta$, whence $g_{q}\ge g_S+(q-S)$ for the decreasing limit
$g_S=\inf_{q>S}g_q$, which is independent of the approximating sequence.
Each terminal pivot is affine in the target label, so $g_S$ is
\emph{concave}; and $g_S$ is the \emph{greatest} Bellman-feasible storage at
level $S$, hence an exact fixed point of the Bellman operator. Concavity is
elementary but decisive: it forces every corner of the contact intercept
downward, which is what ultimately excludes contact plateaus.

\emph{Exact endpoint exclusion.} Two-edge finite histories give exact rational
margins: with $r=1/10$, the endpoint predecessor lines exceed the storage
uniformly by
$m_+=\tfrac{23686917837403}{3008753881083980}>0.0403$ and
$m_-=\tfrac{274562305945801}{4008753881083980}>0.1978$,
for every level in the certified window. Consequently the remainder $R_0$ has
a uniform positive gap on all endpoint spectral sectors, so no exact or
near-maximizing state carries endpoint mass, and $x=\pm1$ is never a Bellman
predecessor at $S$. These are closed-form rational certificates; no branch
geometry or reflected wing is used.

\emph{The equality module of a hypothetical maximizer.} Suppose a
finite-dimensional strategy attains $S$. (Purification is unnecessary:
$\operatorname{Tr}(\rho R)=0$ with $R\succeq0$ gives $R\rho=0$ directly; POVMs
are handled by a finite Naimark dilation preserving dimension, tensor
structure, and value.) Applying the weld at levels $q_n\downarrow S$ and using
$\sum_\nu\operatorname{Tr}(\rho R_{\nu,n})=q_n-S\to0$, the endpoint gaps kill
boundary mass first; on the remaining interior finite spectrum the
coefficients converge, and the limiting positive remainders annihilate
$\rho$. Thus the maximizer carries an exact critical equality module at $S$
--- \emph{without} any claim that the storage infimum is attained (the
endpoint boundary layer is bypassed, not solved).

\emph{Zero-set geometry.} On the equality module, the scalar remainder
symbol vanishes. Reflected finite histories give the gluing inequality
$g_S(x)\,g_S(-x)\ge b(x)^2$ with $b(x)^2=(1-x^2)/4$; full equality of the two
Bellman inequalities and the Cauchy--Schwarz step forces
$g_S(x)g_S(-x)=b(x)^2$ at every occupied pair, and a Monge (supermodularity)
argument makes the full zero set monotone. The decisive step is a
convex-envelope theorem: writing $C(x)=S+1-\tfrac{x}{2}-b(x)^2/g_S(x)$ on the
open interval $(-1,1)$ --- where $C\ge g_S(0)>0$ by feasibility at the origin,
so the greatest convex minorant $H$ exists --- concavity of $g_S$ makes every
corner of $C$ point downward, while a kink of $H$ at a contact point would
force an upward corner. Hence $H$ is differentiable, no source serves two
targets, and (by an exact involution $(x,u)\mapsto(-u,-x)$ of the constraint
system, which requires no symmetry of $g_S$ itself) no target is served by two
sources. The occupied support of any maximizer is therefore a strictly
increasing one-to-one graph.

\emph{Finiteness forces closure; closure caps the value.} The equality module
carries two unitary response transports implementing the decreasing
involutions $a(u)=P^{-1}(-P(u))$ and $b(u)=-u$ on the occupied support; the
graph property makes both total. A finite totally ordered set admits exactly
one decreasing bijection, so $a=b$ on the support: every occupied pair has its
reflected partner. The resulting amplitude-ratio elimination (multiplicity-%
uniform: the transports are scalar multiples of unitaries between complete
fibres) yields, for an occupied pair $(x,u)$,
\[
S \;=\; xu-1+\sqrt{(b_x+b_u)^2+\tfrac{(x-u)^2}{4}}
\;\le\; -t+\sqrt{t}\;\le\;\tfrac14,\qquad t=1-xu,
\]
contradicting $S>1/4$. This proves Theorem~\ref{thm:N}. The nonclosure half of
Corollary~\ref{cor:nonclosure} follows by compactness of the behavior space:
finite-dimensional behaviors approach value $S$; a convergent subsequence has
value exactly $S$; closedness of $\Cq$ would give a finite-dimensional
realization attaining $S$.

\begin{remark}
The only role of finite dimension is the single sentence ``a finite ordered
set has one decreasing bijection.'' Everything else holds for arbitrary
maximizers, which is exactly why Theorem~\ref{thm:S} is possible.
\end{remark}

\section{Spatial attainment}\label{sec:S}

The historical route to attainment constructed amplitudes along a bi-infinite
wall and then demanded a global compatibility equation --- the equation whose
exact failure decertified the original release. The present route
\emph{dissolves} that obstruction rather than repairing it: no amplitude is
ever constructed, and the equation is never posed.

By weak-$*$ compactness a maximizing state $\Omega$ exists for the commuting
model (whose value is always attained \cite{NPA}; nonattainment is strictly a
tensor-model phenomenon). The limit passage of Section~\ref{sec:N} applies
verbatim --- no finite spectral support is needed once the transports are
expressed through the exact operator blocks $W=Y(B_3-\tfrac12 I)$ and
$W_B=(A_3-\tfrac12 I)V$, which satisfy $W^2=b(X)^2$ and $WX=-XW$ as projector
identities (no global spectral involution is assumed; it need not exist).
These yield exact Radon--Nikodym transport laws for the scalar spectral
measure $\mu$ of the commuting pair $(X,U)$. The fixed-point set of the
response translation $\tau=a\circ b$ carries no mass (on it the two transport
densities coincide, which fires the quarter-ceiling elimination against
$S>1/4$), so $\mu$ disintegrates over the countable free orbits of $\tau$ via
an explicit Borel transversal. Choosing one conditional orbit measure and
interleaving the weights of $\{u_n\}$ and $\{-u_n\}$ produces amplitudes
$\lambda_j=\sqrt{\mu_t(\{c_j\})}$ with, automatically,
$1\le\sum_j\lambda_j^2\le2$: \emph{normalizability is inherited from the
probability measure, not imposed} --- this is the precise point where the
historical obstruction disappears. The transport laws then \emph{derive} the
cocycle $\lambda_{j+1}/\lambda_j=g_S(c_j)/b(c_j)$, and the Bellman contact
equality closes the Jacobi eigenvalue equation $H\lambda=S\lambda$ exactly.
The finite P\'al--V\'ertesi block identity installs the normalized eigenpair
as a spatial strategy with value $S$, proving Theorem~\ref{thm:S} and, with
Theorem~\ref{thm:N}, the separation in Corollary~\ref{cor:nonclosure}.

\begin{remark}[Asymmetry]
Numerical reconnaissance stably locates the active predecessor range near
$[-0.894,\,+0.898]$: the critical envelope appears genuinely asymmetric, and
no step above obtains the left wing by reflection. This is compatible with the
$u\mapsto-u$ symmetry that the closure argument forces on the \emph{occupied
orbit} of a hypothetical finite maximizer: envelope and orbit are different
objects. The historical construction's implicit reflection symmetry is,
in retrospect, adjacent to the exact incompatibility that decertified it.
\end{remark}

\section{Discussion: attainment and open carriers}\label{sec:discussion}

The two theorems exhibit a sharp mechanism: \emph{finiteness forces the
equality transports to close (return), and closure caps the value strictly
below $S$; the value above the closed ceiling is carried only by an infinite
open chain}. It is natural to ask how general this picture is. A survey of
the known zoo is suggestive. Every Bell functional with proven
finite-dimensional attainment of its global supremum closes for an
identifiable algebraic reason: bipartite correlator (XOR) functionals via
Tsirelson's Clifford construction \cite{Tsirelson,CHTW} (anticommutation is
two-step closure), all $(2,2;2,2)$ functionals via Jordan's lemma
\cite{Masanes}, chained inequalities (rank-2 Gram), fixed CGLMP functionals
(attained at local dimension exactly $d$; proven for $d=3,4$
\cite{IoannouRosset}), and the SOS-certified families --- a sum-of-squares
certificate being precisely a dimension-free algebraic closure of the
optimum. Conversely, both known explicit transport-class nonattainment
objects are infinite open ladders: the Coladangelo--Stark state is an exactly
geometric ladder whose nonattainment proof is an explicit no-return orbit
argument on the Schmidt spectrum \cite{ColadangeloStark}, and the
P\'al--V\'ertesi family is a half-infinite Jacobi chain with offset $2\times2$
blockings \cite{PalVertesi} --- the same architecture, on smaller alphabets,
now with the conjecture it motivated resolved. (Coladangelo and Stark
explicitly suggested their orbit technique might apply to $I_{3322}$;
Theorem~\ref{thm:N} may be read as the answer to that suggestion, reached by
a transfer-operator route.) Embezzlement-based nonattainment
\cite{vanDamHayden,JLV,Coladangelo} is carried by the log-flat ladder
$p_j\propto1/j$ --- open but null-recurrent rather than transient, matching
its distinct operator-algebraic classification \cite{vLSWW}. Group-theoretic
and type-based obstructions \cite{Slofstra1,DPP,MusatRordam,MIPRE} form a
third family outside this transport picture, and Slofstra's undecidability
results imply any general structural criterion must be restricted to a
subclass. Within the two-outcome bipartite class, where Jordan's lemma
supplies the $2\times2$ chain structure, we record the dichotomy as a
conjecture: \emph{finite-dimensional attainment coincides with finite
recurrence of the critical transport}. A concrete test: the
Dykema--Paulsen--Prakash $(5,5;2,2)$ nonclosure \cite{DPP} is proved
non-constructively; the conjecture predicts a forced-open orbit in its
critical transport. We also note the marginal/correlator boundary: Tsirelson's
theorem covers correlators only, and it is exactly the marginal terms through
which $I_{3322}$ escapes it. Finally, two cautions from the literature: the
commuting value is always attained \cite{NPA} (so nonattainment is a
tensor-side phenomenon), and non-saturation of NPA levels is not evidence of
nonattainment \cite{DTCHSH,NPAnonattain}.

What remains open here: the exact identification of $S$ beyond the certified
window (the window's width is $3.61\times10^{-9}$), and the matching
dimension-necessity lower bound (how fast $S-S_d\to0$ from below); a
constructive upper profile with logarithmic dimension sufficiency is part of
ongoing work.

\section{Methods and verification}\label{sec:methods}

All load-bearing computations are exact (rational arithmetic or outward-%
rounded interval arithmetic), two-engine where numerical (independent
reconstructions in separate codebases; e.g.\ the $255$-dimensional lower
strategy is verified in exact rationals and reproduced at $160$ digits), and
shipped as machine-checkable certificates in the public repository
\cite{Repo}, with the complete correction record. The proof texts themselves
were audited by independent refutation-first adversarial review: three
successive rounds for Theorem~\ref{thm:N} (the first round \emph{refuted} an
earlier restoration attempt with an explicit countermodel; the third signed
the theorem with a binding claim boundary that this paper follows), and a
separate round for Theorem~\ref{thm:S} (which located and repaired two
genuine gaps --- a circular fixed-point exclusion and a globally nonexistent
spectral involution --- before signing). The complete signed verdicts ship in
the certificate directories. Package guard scripts are algebraic and exact-%
arithmetic checks only and are labeled as such; the analytic steps are
established in the reviewed documents, not by the scripts.
