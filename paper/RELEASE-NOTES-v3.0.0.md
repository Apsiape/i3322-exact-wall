# Release notes — v3.0.0 (2026-08-05)

## Headline

**Finite-dimensional nonattainment and nonclosure are restored.** At the
common quantum value `S` of the I3322 functional (Collins–Gisin
normalization: classical bound `0`, qubit/qutrit maximum exactly `1/4`),
certified to lie in `(0.2508753845015185, 0.250875388108398]`:

- **Theorem (N).** No finite-dimensional quantum strategy — any dimension,
  pure or mixed states, projective or POVM measurements — attains `S`.
- **Corollary.** `C_q(3,3;2,2)` is not closed.

This is the operative content of the Pál–Vértesi conjecture (2010). The
proof route is new and independent of the decertified Sprint-1195
amplitude normalization: a limiting-weld substitute, exact rational
endpoint receipts, storage concavity with a reflection-gluing defect
inequality, zero-set localization, and an open-interval convex-envelope
no-kink theorem, feeding the Sprint-1198 equality-kernel mechanism.

## Review record

The proof survived three rounds of independent refutation-first
adversarial review. Round 1 refuted an earlier restoration attempt with an
explicit countermodel. Round 2 validated the underlying mechanism and
found one of four required certificate inputs in place. Round 3 audited
the completed chain line by line, repaired one written-proof gap
(the convex-minorant domain), independently replicated the finite
Pál–Vértesi block identity, and signed the theorem with a binding public
claim boundary. The full round-3 verdict ships in
`certificate/production/theorem-N-four-receipts-at-S/review/ROUND3-VERDICT.md`.

## Claim boundary (binding)

- `S` is identified ONLY by the certified window (width `3.61e-9`). It is
  not asserted to equal the historical decimal, and the historical
  construction is not asserted to be optimal.
- Spatial attainment at `S` and the `C_qs \ C_q` separation are OPEN and
  not claimed.
- The dimension-necessity lower bound remains open.
- Package artifact scripts are algebraic/exact-arithmetic guards, not
  theorem verifiers; the logical steps live in the reviewed documents.
