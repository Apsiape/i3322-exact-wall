# Release notes — v4.0.0 (2026-08-25)

The paper-consolidation release: one paper, one claim set, one citation.

## The merge

The standalone rate note (`paper/rate-companion.tex`, shipped at v3.3.0)
has been folded into the main paper as **Section 5 (Dimension
complexity)** of `paper/resolution.tex`. The mathematical content is
unchanged — same theorem, same constants, same certificate anchors at
`certificate/production/rate-theta-log/` — and the standalone note is
preserved verbatim in the frozen v3.3.0 release. `rate-companion.tex` is
now a supersession notice pointing at Section 5.

Why: the rate result is the quantitative completion of Theorem (N), and
keeping the two statements in separate documents produced avoidable
drift between them (several instances found and fixed in this cycle:
"normal spatial" vs "spatial", a qutrit-maximum claim the main paper
deliberately does not make, opposite DOI-pinning conventions, and stale
residual-risk language for a risk discharged by the 2026-08-07
sections-6-9 amendment).

## Paper (resolution.tex, rebuilt PDF, now 16 pp)

Beyond the merge, this cycle's multi-reader adversarial review (five
independent readers on the pre-merge paper, three on the merged one, a
certificate-evidence audit, and primary-source checks against
Pal-Vertesi 1006.3032, Coladangelo-Stark 1804.05116 (v1 and published),
and Coladangelo 1904.02350) produced the following corrections, all
applied:

- **Theorem (S) restated with its locality constraint** (projections on
  each factor, A_i = a_i (x) I, B_j = I (x) b_j) and the explicit
  bipartite vector psi_S = sum_j lambda_j e_j (x) e_j; previously the
  statement did not formally require the measurements to split across
  the tensor factors.
- **Section 4's orbit structure corrected**: the disintegration is over
  the infinite-dihedral group generated by the two response transports
  (orbits may carry nontrivial stabilizers), not over "free orbits" of
  the response translation; the transversal is constructed (first
  rational separating an orbit point from its image) with the
  monotonicity of tau — a composition of two decreasing maps — stated,
  and the a.e.-to-pointwise selection of the conditional orbit measure
  made explicit.
- **Pal-Vertesi positioning corrected against the primary source**:
  their conjecture is a single claim (the parenthetical is a
  definition), which Theorem (N) proves entire; their separate assertion
  of infinite-dimensional attainment is now quoted and addressed by
  Theorem (S).
- **Minimality argument completed**: two-input scenarios (2,n;2,2) are
  proved closed for every n via Jordan's lemma plus Schmidt-rank
  compression, so three inputs per party is the smallest possible
  two-outcome venue; the survey now separates two-outcome from
  larger-alphabet witnesses and includes the embezzlement-based
  non-closure proof.
- **Priority for the rate claim narrowed and sourced**: the paper now
  quotes the Coladangelo-Stark arXiv-v1 remark on quantitative dimension
  bounds (absent from the published version), concedes the routine
  truncation upper bound for their witness, and claims only a first
  *proved* two-sided characterization for a witness of the C_q-inside-
  C_qs separation, on a negative search — evidence, not a theorem of
  priority. `paper/PRIORITY-AUDIT.md` carries the corresponding
  amendment (2026-08-25).
- Numerous statement-level repairs: symbol collisions resolved (the
  Bell operator vs the bounded-operator algebra; the gluing function b
  vs the reflection involution, now sigma; the convex minorant, now
  \check C; the Jacobi operator, now J), GNS step made explicit, the
  transport-block operators X, U, Y, V and the joint spectral measure E
  defined, near-maximizer endpoint-mass bound stated correctly
  (eps/m, not zero), the Jordan's-lemma sentence in the Discussion
  corrected (pairwise structure, not a three-projection decomposition),
  the Pi_2-completeness statement attached to the decision problem, the
  tilted-CHSH caution scoped to the critical line, the closing
  observation restated in the correct direction (attained implies
  finitely recurrent — the converse is open), and the methods section
  reduced to exact-computation and language-model-use statements.
- All constants in Section 5 re-verified at 50-digit precision:
  12/12 checks pass, zero unsafe roundings; one display (y^2 bound)
  corrected from 0.9197272 to 0.91972725 during the cycle, and the
  derivation of K_0 now states explicitly that it routes through the
  exact decaying root, not the rounded display.
